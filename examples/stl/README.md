# Example meshes (STL)

Own geometry, made by `cargo run -p rolidium-mesh --example make_examples`; released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).

* `plate-with-hole.stl` (binary) and `plate-with-hole-ascii.stl`: a plate 60 x 40 x 10 mm with a round hole of 5 mm diameter. Try: `import-stl examples/stl/plate-with-hole.stl`, `mesh-regions`, `mesh-pull 1 5`, `mesh-hole 1 6`.
* `cube-20.stl`: a cube of 20 mm.
